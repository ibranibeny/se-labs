# Fabric Networking & Landing Zone Integration

> **Draft asset.** Overview extracted from the *Cloud & AI — SE Accelerators* deck
> (May 2026). Full hands-on content is being developed.

A half-day workshop to prepare a **Microsoft Fabric** tenant for pilot/MVP use cases by
integrating it with your Azure landing zone and securing data access.

## What it covers

- Fabric **inbound** networking
- Fabric **outbound** networking
- Fabric **landing-zone integration**
- Preparation of the Fabric tenant for pilot / MVP use cases

Fabric connects to private clouds (Azure Virtual Networks or on-premises data centers)
primarily via **Azure Private Link**, **Managed Private Endpoints**, and **Data Gateways**,
keeping data traffic on Microsoft's private backbone rather than the public internet.

## Audience & outcome

For infra / cloud / network admins and Fabric admins. Outcome: a Fabric tenant prepared
for access and data-source ingestion (Sales Stage 3).
