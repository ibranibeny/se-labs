# AI in a Day — AI Citadel Governance Platform

> **Draft asset.** Overview extracted from the *Cloud & AI — SE Accelerators* deck
> (May 2026). Full hands-on content, repo, and pricing pack are being developed.

A one-day, hands-on workshop to deploy and configure the **Foundry Citadel Platform
Landing Zone** — a pre-configured central governance and AI apps/agents landing zone
that accelerates AI deployment while keeping it secure, governed, and production-ready.

## Why customers need it

Across industries the same concerns surface: **agent sprawl** and resource access,
**shadow AI** and new threats (indirect prompt injection), **data oversharing &
leakage**, and **regulatory compliance**. 88% of leaders cite sensitive-data leakage as
their main concern and 80% lack understanding of how AI is regulated.

## Three pillars

- **Governance & Security** — centralized governance of MCPs, LLMs, and AI backends; a Universal AI Registry; policy controls enforced at scale via a universal AI gateway (APIM).
- **Observability & Compliance** — AI evaluation checks, performance & cost optimization, early issue detection, audit-ready logging aligned to ISO 27001 / SOC 2 / EU AI Act.
- **AI Development Velocity** — democratize AI development with low-code & pro-code agent templates and reliable blueprints.

## Agenda (subject to change)

- Use-case deep-dive and architecture walkthrough
- **Lab 1** — Deployment of the Citadel landing zone
- **Lab 2** — AI Gateway (APIM) configuration
- **Lab 3** — Security & compliance controls (Purview, Defender, Entra)
- Grab-and-go pack: timeline, pricing, repo handover

## Audience & outcome

For developers, security, DevOps, and TDMs. Expected outcomes: **zero-trust by default**,
~30–40% lower TCO, 50% faster model onboarding, and a standardized blueprint that anchors
Azure AI, AOAI, and Foundry consumption while pulling in adjacent AKS, Storage, and Monitor workloads.
